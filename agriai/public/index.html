An answer, in plain words

A farmer asks the way they would ask a neighbour. AgriAI replies with context pulled from its knowledge base.

My tomato leaves are curling and turning yellow. What should I do?

Curling with yellowing usually points to one of three things: a viral infection spread by whiteflies, uneven watering, or a nutrient shortage.

Start by checking the underside of the leaves for tiny white insects. Then look at the soil: if it stays soggy or dries out completely between waterings, steady the schedule. If the older leaves yellow first, a balanced fertilizer may help.

This is general guidance. For a severe outbreak, please consult a local agricultural expert.

Retrieval first, then the language model

The model never searches the knowledge base itself. The backend finds the relevant context and hands it over with your question, which keeps answers grounded in agricultural information.

  1. You ask

    A question in natural language through the web interface.

  2. The backend listens

    Node.js and Express process the query and identify the farming context.

  3. The knowledge base responds

    Relevant facts on crops, pests, diseases, fertilizers and irrigation are retrieved.

  4. The model writes the answer

    The language model turns the question and the retrieved context into a clear reply.
